# Anime Action References

## BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War — Action Production Interview
- Source: https://bleach-anime.com/special/interview03/29.html
- Study: the production team explains how difficult action layouts are solved with reference photography and dedicated action animators.
- Movement design: complex battle ideas are first clarified through physical staging and camera-angle tests before animation polish.
- Camera / editing: challenging perspectives are planned with real-world spatial references rather than invented blindly.
- AI-video takeaway: for difficult AI fight shots, treat the prompt like previs: define floor plan, camera side, fighter positions and one clear action beat before adding speed or effects.
- Tags: anime-production, action-layout, previs, camera-angle, spatial-planning

## ONE PIECE FILM RED — Battle Animation Interview
- Source: https://www.onepiece-film.jp/interview/
- Study: the official production interview discusses the difficulty of making complex manga-style sword and battle actions readable in animation.
- Movement design: the team balances physically understandable action with selective abbreviation and effects so the sequence remains fast without becoming incoherent.
- Camera / editing: effects and shot design are used to compress transitions that would otherwise slow the action.
- AI-video takeaway: when prompting exaggerated anime combat, do not describe every transitional movement. Keep the key contact poses and use controlled motion blur or effects to bridge only the fastest transitions.
- Tags: anime-action, swordplay, effects, readability, motion-compression
