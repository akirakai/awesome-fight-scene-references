# Stunt & Choreography References

## The Raid 2 — Choreography Behind the Scenes
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qXecmLI12GY
- Study: direct behind-the-scenes material showing how intricate Pencak Silat screen choreography is rehearsed and staged.
- Movement design: continuous attack/counter logic, tight hand fighting, progressive escalation and clear transitions between empty-hand and weapon work.
- Camera / editing: choreography is designed first as a continuous physical flow, then broken into shots while preserving screen direction and exhaustion progression.
- AI-video takeaway: build fight prompts as a choreography arc: technical exchange → escalating pressure → fatigue → rough desperate finish. This produces more believable progression than stacking random spectacular moves.
- Tags: pencak-silat, rehearsal, choreography, behind-the-scenes, grounded-action

## Creed — Single-Take Boxing Fight
- Source: https://nofilmschool.com/2015/12/anatomy-scene-ryan-coogler-narrates-single-take-fight-scene-creed
- Study: a boxing match staged around the performer and Steadicam operator as one synchronized choreography system.
- Movement design: boxing combinations, clinches, knockdowns and corner transitions are timed around camera position rather than treated as independent action.
- Camera / editing: extended uninterrupted Steadicam movement changes angle and distance inside the ring while maintaining spatial clarity.
- AI-video takeaway: for a believable AI oner, choreograph the camera as another participant: orbit, cross behind a fighter, settle into profile for an exchange, then push closer after a decisive hit.
- Tags: boxing, oner, steadicam, camera-choreography, spatial-continuity

## Kingsman: The Secret Service — Church Fight BTS
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G9sy_qwhjm8
- Study: behind-the-scenes look at a highly stylized fight designed to feel like a continuous shot despite complex staging and transitions.
- Movement design: rapid target switching, environmental interaction and rhythmic chains of short action ideas.
- Camera / editing: aggressive camera travel, whip movement and concealed transitions maintain apparent continuity while constantly changing composition.
- AI-video takeaway: useful for hyper-kinetic sequences where the camera should connect several micro-fights without losing the protagonist. Prompt a clear subject lock plus motivated whip transitions between nearby threats.
- Tags: hyperkinetic, faux-oner, whip-pan, environmental-combat, stunt-choreography

## The Raid 2 — Kitchen Fight Design
- Source: https://dailydead.com/exclusive-interview-raid-2s-iko-uwais/
- Study: Iko Uwais describes roughly six weeks of preparation and a choreography that evolves from hand-to-hand exchanges into small-knife combat.
- Movement design: the scene has an explicit progression rather than a flat stream of moves; technical exchanges gradually become more desperate and damaging.
- Camera / editing: short shot pieces still preserve a continuous sense of physical exhaustion and escalating stakes.
- AI-video takeaway: define phases in longer generations: controlled parry/counter exchange → weapon introduction → fatigue → brutal final beat. Keep each phase visually distinct.
- Tags: pencak-silat, knife-fight, escalation, fatigue, choreography-arc

## ONE PIECE Live Action Season 2 — Behind the Stunts
- Source: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/videos/one-piece-season-2-behind-the-stunts
- Study: an official stunt featurette showing how the returning stunt team scales up live-action battles while keeping recognizable character movement.
- Movement design: highly stylized attacks are grounded by performer training, practical rehearsal and clear body mechanics before spectacle is layered on top.
- Camera / editing: useful reference for blending larger-than-life character action with readable live-action staging.
- AI-video takeaway: when adapting anime-like movement into photorealistic AI video, first define a believable physical base action, then add only one exaggerated signature beat rather than making every motion superhuman.
- Tags: live-action-anime, stunt-team, adaptation, character-movement, grounded-spectacle
