## film-TV

### The Protector / Tom-Yum-Goong — Staircase One-Take Fight
- Category: Film / Muay Thai / One-Take Group Fight
- Direct watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bqw369ZskMk
- Alternate scene page: https://www.tvguide.com/videos/the-protector/2000138646/the-protector-scene-staircase/3595171/
- Why study it: One of the clearest examples of choreography designed around architecture. The action climbs a multi-level spiral space while Tony Jaa keeps moving toward a simple objective.
- Action design: advance to next landing → one or two attackers enter → short decisive exchange → attacker is displaced / removed → protagonist continues upward → next threat enters from a new elevation or side.
- Camera / editing: A roughly four-minute Steadicam long take keeps the geography continuous. The camera follows behind, beside, and around the performer without resetting the space through cuts.
- AI-video takeaway: For a generated oner, define environmental waypoints. Use staircase landing A → rail turn → landing B → doorway → upper level, and assign one primary action beat to each waypoint. Continuous forward objective is more stable than "fight many enemies dynamically."
- Tags: Tony-Jaa, Muay-Thai, oner, staircase, group-fight, environment-choreography, waypoint
