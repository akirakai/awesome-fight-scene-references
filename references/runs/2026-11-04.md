## Suggested 15-second exercise

1. **0–3 s:** Establish the opponents, any contested object and the screen axis (wide, stable).
2. **3–7 s:** Complete one clear attack → response → recover sequence; keep both fighters and contact point visible.
3. **7–11 s:** Use a single motivated relocation or environmental constraint (side-step, object retrieval, safe-zone movement); preserve positions across any cut.
4. **11–15 s:** Close with one counter/action consequence and balanced finish. Review limb/weapon ownership, support-foot placement and the continuity of velocity.

The prompts are deliberately original abstractions instead of requests to replicate copyrighted characters, shots or exact choreography verbatim.
