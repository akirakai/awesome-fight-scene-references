A curated batch of non-duplicate fight/action references selected for choreography clarity, camera value, and usefulness in AI-video prompting.

## Film / TV

### Mortal Kombat II — “GET OVER HERE!” Scorpion Clip
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ao6Xs-0cmr0
- Additional action featurette: https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/mortal_kombat_ii/videos/o8z72eedblFx
- Published: 2026-04-24 (clip)
- Study: a concise live-action translation of an iconic game move, useful for studying how a signature attack is staged as a complete visual sentence rather than a vague burst of action.
- Movement design: clear setup → committed ranged/weapon action → forced opponent displacement → strong finishing pose. The action reads because the signature beat has an obvious beginning, contact event and result.
- Camera / editing: the framing prioritizes silhouette and attack direction; spectacle is concentrated around the signature move instead of keeping every frame equally busy.
- AI-video takeaway: when adapting a “special move,” lock three anchors—recognizable preparation pose → one explicit trajectory/contact event → opponent reaction/hero recovery pose. Add effects only around the contact window.
- Tags: mortal-kombat-ii, scorpion, signature-move, weapon-trajectory, live-action-game-adaptation, 2026

## Games

### Fable — Official Quest Gameplay / First Combat Demo at gamescom 2026
- Watch: https://youtu.be/trRwKq5FppU
- Combat breakdown: https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/08/26/fable-combat-first-demo-explained/
- Published: 2026-08-26
- Study: fresh extended gameplay showing seamless switching between melee, ranged weapons and magic while fighting several enemies.
- Movement design: the useful pattern is not “use everything at once,” but tactical state chaining—impede/control a group with a spell → stagger or isolate with ranged fire → close distance → melee finisher. Parries, dodges and weapon finishers provide visible punctuation between modes.
- Camera / editing: the third-person camera stays stable while the combat vocabulary changes, so mode transitions remain readable even during multi-enemy pressure.
- AI-video takeaway: excellent state-machine template for fantasy action. Write `magic control → ranged stagger → melee close → finisher`, with one explicit transition frame between modes. Keep the camera comparatively stable while the character changes combat state.
- Tags: fable, gamescom-2026, melee, ranged, magic, state-transition, multi-enemy, finisher

- Published: 2026-08-19
- Study: sci-fi guerrilla warfare against alien invaders in a recognizable urban environment, useful for studying how large-scale danger is kept legible through localized character beats.

## Martial Arts / Performance

### Kajornklai Sor Sor Toipadriew vs. Tewadalek — ONE Friday Fights 167
- Full event: https://watch.onefc.com/events/one-friday-fights-167
- Event date: 2026-08-21
- Study: current elite Muay Thai footage with neutral broadcast framing; Kajornklai stopped Tewadalek by KO in round two, making it useful for studying how a real fight can shift abruptly from measured exchange to decisive finish.
- Movement design: natural probing distance, guarded exchanges and reset behavior precede the high-confidence finishing window. The contrast between low-intensity reading and sudden commitment is the useful reference—not merely the knockout itself.
- Camera / editing: fixed sports coverage preserves both full bodies, distance and the contact line, making weight shift and response timing much easier to inspect than in cinematic action.
- AI-video takeaway: use `probe/feint → defend/reset → recognize opening → short committed burst → decisive reaction`. Do not make the entire 15 seconds equally aggressive.
- Tags: muay-thai, one-friday-fights-167, distance-management, burst, guard-reset, realistic-combat, 2026

## AI Action

### DualFlow — Unified Interactive + Reactive Two-Person Motion Generation (ICLR 2026)
- Project / demos: https://gprerit96.github.io/dualflow-page/
- ICLR paper: https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/a783b0bba6aaa9dec34aca6500d853cd-Abstract-Conference.html
- Study: DualFlow explicitly unifies synchronized two-person interaction generation with reactive generation where Person B responds to Person A, rather than treating the two motion tracks independently.
- Movement design: the framework conditions on text, music and prior motion, while synchronization objectives improve interpersonal timing; its reactive mode is especially relevant to fight generation because one person’s motion becomes the causal input to the other.
- Camera / editing: research visualizations remove cinematic distraction and make timing, spacing and response coherence easy to diagnose.
- AI-video takeaway: model a fight as alternating control modes: `A leads / B reacts → brief synchronized contact → B leads / A reacts → reset`. Explicitly state who is leading each window and preserve identity across role changes.
- Tags: dualflow, iclr-2026, two-person-motion, reactive-motion, interactive-motion, temporal-coupling, ai-action
