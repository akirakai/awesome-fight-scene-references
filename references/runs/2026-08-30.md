# Curated Fight References — 2026-08-30

New cases screened against the existing category files and prior dated runs. This batch favors directly watchable sources, clear physical causality, readable camera grammar, and patterns that can be translated into 10–15 second AI-video prompts.

## Film / TV

### Monkey Man — Kitchen Fight
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_1PuVDh14h4
- Type: live-action / environmental close-quarters fight.
- Action design: compact strike-and-grapple exchanges are chained through utensils, counters, glass and hard surfaces; props solve tactical problems instead of functioning as decoration.
- Camera / editing: energetic close coverage stays oriented to the protagonist and the current attacker; cuts generally coincide with contact or target changes.
- AI-video takeaway: **threat A → prop interaction → visible contact/consequence → target switch → improvised finish**. Never trigger breakage before the body or weapon visibly reaches the object.

### Novocaine — Kitchen Fight
- Watch: https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/novocaine_2025/videos/2422917699585
- Type: live-action / comedic hard-contact environmental fight.
- Action design: burns, glass, blunt impacts and kitchen hazards repeatedly alter body position and tactical state. The character’s pain immunity changes expression and decision-making, but it does not cancel momentum or physical damage.
- Camera / editing: impacts are allowed enough screen time for cause, displacement and environmental consequence to register before the next beat.
- AI-video takeaway: separate physics from emotion: **contact → body displacement → environmental consequence → muted pain reaction → immediate counter**. Useful for invulnerable, armored or superhuman characters.

### Ghost Killer — Official Fight Clip
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VlAHZbNH7i4
- Type: live-action / Japanese close-quarters action.
- Action design: compact punches, elbows, kicks, evasions and abrupt direction changes give a smaller female lead speed without making her weightless or implausibly strong.
- Camera / editing: medium and medium-close coverage frequently keeps hips, shoulders and the contact line visible; speed comes from timing and commitment rather than frantic cutting.
- AI-video takeaway: excellent photoreal female-action template: **entry → short attack/counter phrase → recovery foot placement → re-engagement**. Preserve torso rotation and planted-foot mechanics.

## Games

### Stranger Than Heaven — Hands-On Combat / RGG Roundup
- Watch: https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21117-new-stranger-than-heaven-combat-gameplay-video-rgg
- Type: game combat / grounded brawler.
- Action design: the August 2026 presentation exposes a deliberate left/right-body control scheme, light/heavy attacks, blocks, counters and weapons, making attack direction unusually explicit.
- Camera / editing: observational gameplay framing makes it easy to read which side initiates the strike, the defender’s response and the reset instead of manufacturing intensity with cuts.
- AI-video takeaway: **left-side probe → right-side heavy commitment → block/counter → stagger → reset**. Explicitly assigning attack side is a useful way to reduce symmetric or random limb motion in generated fights.

## Anime

### JUJUTSU KAISEN Season 3 — Sendai Colony Trailer
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0BHB_6KPP38
- Type: anime / supernatural multi-fighter battle.
- Action design: the Yuta Sendai Colony preview compresses a multi-opponent encounter into readable threat handoffs—one fighter becomes the immediate threat while another interrupts or changes the state.

- AI-video takeaway: for 3+ fighters use **one active pair + one interrupter**. Keep attack direction consistent across cuts and reserve the largest effect for a readable contact/pose anchor.

## Martial Arts / Performance

### 17th World Wushu Championships — Wang Yawen Women’s Jianshu Gold Medal
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qg78PO68HpU
- Type: real martial-arts performance / straight sword taolu.
- Action design: elite jianshu exposes foot placement, center-of-gravity changes, hip/shoulder rotation, wrist articulation, sword-tip trajectories, leaps, balances and clean recovery.
- Camera / editing: neutral full-body competition coverage preserves the entire body and weapon arc, making the footage especially useful for motion-reference extraction.
- AI-video takeaway: build sword motion from the body outward: **stance/weight shift → hip and shoulder turn → wrist and sword-tip arc → landing/recovery**. Add hair, cloth, wind and cinematic camera motion only after the weapon trajectory is stable.

## Reusable prompt patterns from this batch

- **Environmental fight:** threat → physical prop contact → prop reaction → target switch → new station.
- **Grounded brawl:** directional probe → committed attack → block/counter → displacement → reset.
- **Multi-fighter anime:** active attacker + active defender → one short interrupter → state change → new active pair.
- **Sword performance:** weight transfer → torso rotation → wrist/weapon trajectory → balance/landing → recovery.

The strongest 15-second references in this batch are **Ghost Killer** for a realistic female lead, **Monkey Man** for prop-driven environmental fighting, and **Wang Yawen’s jianshu** for clean sword-body mechanics.
