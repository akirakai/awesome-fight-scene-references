- Action breakdown: https://www.menshealth.com/entertainment/a71508519/masters-of-the-universe-action-scene-snake-mountain/
- Study: a large-scale 1-vs-many fantasy fight designed around a physically dominant hero, heavy sword swings and continuous forward pressure.

### PINO — Person-Interaction Noise Optimization
- Project / video demos: https://sinc865.github.io/pino/
- Study: the project shows generated two-person boxing and multi-person interaction examples while focusing on adding new people without destroying spacing or interaction consistency.
