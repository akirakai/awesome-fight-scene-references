# Curated Fight References — 2026-08-25

Eight non-duplicate references selected for AI video generation. Priority: readable attack/response logic, useful camera grammar, spatial continuity, and promptable action beats.

## Film / TV

### Ip Man 3 — Elevator Fight
- Category: Film / TV — confined-space martial arts
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b_fKzher8QQ
- Why study it: a very small elevator forces the choreography to stay compact and readable; Ip Man also has to protect a second person, which creates clear positional constraints.
- Action design: short Wing Chun parries and counters, body turns, low-line kicks and rapid reorientation rather than large acrobatic movement.
- Camera / editing: medium-close framing uses the elevator walls and doorway as stable spatial anchors; cuts follow tactical reversals rather than every individual hit.
- AI-video takeaway: constrain the room first. Prompt defender position, protected person, wall/door contact and only 3–4 compact attack-response beats.
- Tags: elevator, wing-chun, confined-space, protection, close-quarters, donnie-yen

### The Villainess — Motorcycle Sword Fight
- Category: Film / TV — moving-vehicle sword fight
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ecKc5pOEWk
- Why study it: the sequence combines sword contact, motorcycles and a camera that travels under/around moving vehicles while preserving the protagonist.
- Action design: pursuit first, then side-by-side blade exchanges, position changes between bikes and brief contact beats that are motivated by vehicle geometry.
- Camera / editing: low traveling angles, aggressive forward motion and hidden transitions sell a pseudo-continuous chase without losing the action axis.
- AI-video takeaway: separate three trajectories before prompting: hero bike path, enemy bike path and camera path. Attach only one sword interaction to each relative-position change.
- Tags: motorcycle, swordfight, chase, low-angle, hidden-cut, moving-camera

### John Wick: Chapter 4 — Top-Down Shootout
- Category: Film / TV — overhead gun-fu / environment map
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pksOSHCKp2k
- Production notes: https://www.lionsgateawards.com/assets/files/JW4_Production_Notes.pdf
- Why study it: a near-orthographic overhead camera turns a complicated room-to-room action sequence into an extremely readable spatial map.
- Action design: enter room → clear immediate threat → cross doorway → acquire next threat → use environment/weapon state → continue; each micro-problem is solved before the next begins.
- Camera / editing: the overhead view is unusually stable, so walls, doors and enemy positions provide constant geography while action moves underneath the camera.
- AI-video takeaway: for complex multi-room action, keep the camera simple and let the characters move. Prompt a floor-plan path and one target per room rather than simultaneously complex camera and body motion.
- Tags: john-wick, top-down, gun-fu, spatial-continuity, room-to-room, overhead-camera

## Stunt / Choreography

### Oldboy — Park Chan-wook Breaks Down the Corridor Fight
- Category: Stunt / choreography — director breakdown
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JFfwgv8b3kY
- Why study it: Park Chan-wook explains the corridor fight as a deliberately imperfect, exhausting long action shot rather than a polished stream of perfect hits.
- Action design: lateral crowd pressure, pauses, failed movements, fatigue and re-engagement make the sequence feel progressively messier and more desperate.
- Camera / editing: a side-on wide corridor composition behaves almost like a side-scrolling game, keeping the whole group readable while the protagonist moves through the mass.
- AI-video takeaway: realism can come from imperfection. Add misses, hesitation, stumbling recovery and breath pauses; keep one lateral camera axis instead of adding extra coverage.
- Tags: oldboy, corridor, long-take, side-on, fatigue, crowd-fight, director-breakdown

## Games

### Invincible VS — Official Launch Trailer / 3v3 Tag Combat
- Category: Games — tag fighting / multi-character combat
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K5Ikum6jFho
- Design source: https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/04/30/invincible-vs-launch/
- Why study it: the 3v3 system preserves character identity by keeping one main attacker and defender while assists and swaps occur as short, explicit events.
- Action design: primary pair exchange → assist appears for one beat → state change / launch → tag or reset. Different characters retain distinct power and pressure profiles.
- Camera / editing: the fighting-game camera stays stable during normal combat and becomes cinematic only for major supers or finishers.
- AI-video takeaway: for 3+ characters, never make everyone active at once. Keep one active pair, allow one brief interrupter/assist, then hand focus back cleanly.
- Tags: invincible-vs, tag-fighter, 3v3, assist, character-identity, active-pair

## Anime

### THE RIBBON HERO — Official Trailer (2026)
- Category: Anime — fantasy sword / transformation action
- Watch: https://www.netflix.com/title/81493703
- Production / release source: https://about.netflix.com/ja/news/the-ribbon-hero-keyart
- Why study it: Yuki Igarashi's action-oriented animation uses clear silhouettes and stylized transformation/action poses while keeping the hero readable through fantasy effects.
- Action design: readable ready pose → directional sword action → explicit magical or heroic state change → amplified impact pose.
- Camera / editing: fast cuts are anchored by strong pose changes; effects bridge motion instead of replacing body mechanics entirely.
- AI-video takeaway: lock 3–4 key poses first and treat transformation/VFX as state transitions between them. Do not saturate every frame with equal effect intensity.
- Tags: ribbon-hero, anime, sword, transformation, pose-anchor, fantasy-action, 2026

## Martial Arts

### Banluelok Sitwatcharachai vs. Songchainoi Kiatsongrit — The Inner Circle 27
- Category: Martial Arts — Muay Thai
- Highlights / results: https://www.onefc.com/news/the-inner-circle-27-and-one-friday-fights-167-results-and-highlights-for-every-match/
- Fight report: https://www.onefc.com/news/the-inner-circle-27-main-event-result-banluelok-outworks-songchainoi-to-steal-the-show/
- Date: 2026-08-21
- Why study it: a fresh elite striking reference with repeated left kicks, punch combinations and real defensive recovery under pressure.
- Action design: probe with kick → opponent answers with punches → angle/distance reset → repeat with tactical variation; not every exchange ends in a clean hit.
- Camera / editing: neutral broadcast framing keeps stance, distance and contact line visible, making it useful for short motion-reference extraction.
- AI-video takeaway: use realistic rhythm: probe → short burst → defend/partial miss → angle change → guard reset. Preserve non-contact beats and stance recovery.
- Tags: muay-thai, one-championship, 2026, kick, punch-combination, guard-reset, realistic-combat

## AI Action

### MAGNet — Multi-Agent Diffusion Forcing Transformer
- Category: AI Action — multi-agent interaction / boxing demos
- Project / videos: https://von31.github.io/MAGNet/
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.17900
- Code: https://github.com/Von31/MAGNet-code
- Why study it: MAGNet explicitly models inter-agent coupling and demonstrates long-horizon two-person boxing plus multi-person generation without treating each body as an independent track.
- Movement design: supports partner prediction, joint generation, leader/follower turn-taking and live reaction-style conditioning, exposing several useful interaction-control modes.
- Camera / editing: clean research visualization makes timing, spacing, identity and contact errors visible instead of hiding them behind cinematic cuts.
- AI-video takeaway: think in control modes: A leads/B reacts → synchronized exchange → role handoff → reset. Explicitly choose which fighter is leader/follower in each 2–4 second block.
- Tags: magnet, ai-action, boxing, multi-agent, reaction, leader-follower, interaction-coupling
