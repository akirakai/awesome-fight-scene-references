A curated batch of non-duplicate fight/action references selected for choreography clarity, camera value, and usefulness in AI-video prompting.

## Film / TV

### Flash Point (2007) — Donnie Yen MMA-Style Fight Showcase
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XzaI0YjDC4U
- Source: Hi-YAH! verified channel; the clip showcases *Flash Point* action and credits Donnie Yen, Collin Chou and director Wilson Yip.
- Study: one of the clearest bridges between Hong Kong screen fighting and modern MMA vocabulary, with striking, clinch entries, throws, joint control and ground transitions all visible in one combat language.
- Movement design: exchanges repeatedly change level—stand-up strike → body lock / takedown threat → scramble → recovery—so the fight feels tactical rather than like a flat chain of punches.
- Camera / editing: medium and medium-wide framing keeps hip rotation, stance changes and takedown direction readable; cuts generally reinforce a new tactical phase instead of hiding technique.
- AI-video takeaway: excellent grounded-duel template. Prompt a short sequence as `striking probe → clinch entry → takedown / counter → scramble → stand and reset`, and keep the camera wide enough to preserve both fighters' hips and feet.
- Tags: flash-point, donnie-yen, mma, grappling, takedown, hong-kong-action, grounded-combat, classic

- Published: 2026-08-26
- Study: a fresh Arthurian action reference where Gwendolyn fights directly while summoning spectral Knights of the Round Table as short support attacks.

- Study: current combat footage built around combining physical weapon skill with spells, rituals and temporary otherworldly weapons instead of treating magic as a constant background effect.

## Martial Arts / Performance

### Rodtang vs. Superlek — Full Muay Thai Fight Replay
- Watch: https://www.onefc.com/videos/rodtang-vs-superlek-full-fight-replay-biggest-fight-in-muay-thai/
- Original fight date: 2023-09-22; official replay remains directly watchable from ONE Championship.
- Study: elite real-world striking with extremely clear pressure, guard, distance management, body kicks, elbows, short punching bursts and immediate stance recovery.
- Movement design: neither fighter attacks continuously; probing and guarded pressure create openings, then each committed exchange is short and followed by a visible reset or positional change.
- Camera / editing: broadcast framing keeps both bodies, stance width and contact line visible, which makes it excellent reference for realistic timing and weight transfer.
- AI-video takeaway: use this as a realism baseline: `probe / pressure → one committed 2–3 beat exchange → block / partial hit → angle change → guard reset`. Preserve missed or defended attacks so the fight does not feel pre-programmed.
- Tags: rodtang, superlek, muay-thai, real-fight, pressure, guard-reset, distance-management, full-body-reference
