## martial arts

### Wang Yawen — 17th World Wushu Championships Women’s Jianshu Gold Medal Performance

- Category: Martial Arts / Wushu / Straight Sword
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qg78PO68HpU
- Why it is worth studying: Official IWUF/WUSHU TV competition footage with a clean full-body view and almost no cinematic noise.
- Action design: rooted stance → sword-line preparation → slash/thrust phrase → body turn → traveling step → balance recovery. The weapon path remains tightly coupled to wrist, shoulder, hips and footwork.
- Camera / editing: stable competition framing preserves the full body, ground contact and sword axis throughout the routine.
- AI-video takeaways: strong reference for weapon consistency. Lock the hand-to-hilt relationship, blade axis, foot placement and recovery before adding cloth motion, hair motion or camera movement.
- Tags: wushu, jianshu, straight-sword, weapon-consistency, full-body-reference, official-source

## film-TV

### Flash Point (2007) — Donnie Yen vs. Colin Chou Final Fight

- Category: Film / MMA-influenced Close Quarters
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3LQ55qkw-A
- Alternate source: https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1cW41167vx/
- Why it is worth studying: a landmark screen fight that mixes striking, clinch work, takedowns, positional reversals and hard environmental impacts without losing the attacker/defender relationship.
- Action design: pressure entry → strike exchange → clinch or body lock → takedown / scramble → separation → re-entry. The fight repeatedly changes range instead of staying in one generic combo state.
- Camera / editing: fluid medium coverage often holds enough of the bodies to read hips, shoulders and level changes; cuts usually follow a tactical change rather than hiding every contact.
- AI-video takeaways: useful for realistic 10–15 second MMA-style action. Explicitly prompt range transitions and failed/partial control attempts: strike → clinch → resisted takedown → scramble → reset → counter.
- Tags: flash-point, donnie-yen, mma, grappling, takedown, close-quarters, range-change

## games

### UNDEFEATED: Genesis — Official Demo Launch Trailer

- Category: Games / Superhero Combat / Aerial + Ground
- Source: https://open.video/@ign/v/undefeated-genesis-official-demo-launch-trailer
- Press source: https://press.piratepr.com/undefeated-genesis-launches-demo-and-debuts-new-trailer-featuring-free-flight-superpowered-combat
- Date: 2026-09-20
- Why it is worth studying: the combat is built around traversal momentum. Flight, high-speed closing, ranged powers and ground impact are treated as one connected movement system.
- Action design: distant airborne approach → acceleration → high-speed close → impact or ranged attack → vertical reposition → second engagement. Powers change both combat and traversal state.

- AI-video takeaways: for flying fights, define 3D waypoints and one objective per leg. Example: rooftop-height approach → dive → single impact → rebound upward → ranged counter. Avoid generic “fast aerial battle everywhere.”
- Tags: undefeated-genesis, 2026, superhero, flight, aerial-combat, high-speed, traversal-combat

## anime

### Sword of the Stranger — Nanashi vs. Luo-Lang Final Duel

- Category: Anime / Sword Fight
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JP5uSX1UUtg
- Official full-film source: https://www.crunchyroll.com/series/G24H107GQ/sword-of-the-stranger
- Why it is worth studying: one of the clearest examples of very fast animated swordplay that still preserves intention, footing, blade direction and exhaustion.
- Action design: guard/read → committed cut → deflect or evade → angle change → counter → separation → increasingly desperate re-entry. Speed rises while the duel’s cause-and-effect stays readable.
- Camera / editing: wide and medium-wide shots repeatedly restore geography between aggressive tracking moves and close impact beats; snow and architecture provide stable spatial anchors.
- AI-video takeaways: lock 4–6 anchor poses and screen directions, then connect them with short acceleration bursts. Preserve blade contact and separation states instead of asking for continuous unstructured sword flurries.
- Tags: sword-of-the-stranger, anime, swordfight, anchor-pose, readable-speed, spatial-continuity

## stunt-choreography

### Action Lab — Action Design Reel

- Category: Stunt / Choreography / Action Design Reel
- Source: https://vimeo.com/1155731069
- Why it is worth studying: this is explicitly an action-design reel built around designing, rehearsing and executing physical action for camera, making it cleaner than finished feature-film footage for motion study.
- Action design: short rehearsal-ready phrases with clear initiation, response, contact, displacement and recovery. The movements are designed to survive camera translation instead of relying on VFX to create impact.
