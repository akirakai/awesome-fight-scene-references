## Suggested 15-second original AI action experiment

- **0–3 s:** establish two combatants, a fixed obstacle and one clear motivation in a steady wide shot.
- **3–7 s:** one attacker advances; the other parries or dodges, with contact and reaction visible together.
- **7–11 s:** one measured counter; the first attacker loses balance and reestablishes stance.
- **11–15 s:** either hold on a result or use a single reaction insert; keep character, prop, impact and screen-axis continuity.

**Evaluation:** attack/contact causality; grounded footwork; spatial continuity; prop/weapon ownership; camera contribution; character identity. Re-run the same action with locked camera before trying a moving camera. Any specific timecodes here are proposed test beats, not footage timestamps.
