# Fight Reference Run — 2026-08-18

Eight non-duplicate references selected for practical AI action-video prompting and previs.

## Film / TV

### HAVOC — Fishing-Shack Finale / Hard-Contact Close Quarters
- Watch: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/videos/havoc-trailer
- Production notes: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/tom-hardy-havoc-ending-explained
- Action design: short tactical bursts, hard contact, heavy close-range strikes and an environmental finish.
- Camera / editing: controlled chunks preserve pressure and geography instead of relying on an impossible continuous take.
- AI-video takeaway: build 15 seconds from 2–4 second modules—enter → defend/counter → reposition → environmental finish.

### The Shadow Strays — Warehouse / Multi-Style Assassin Action
- Watch / trailer: https://about.netflix.com/en/news/the-shadow-strays-trailer-tease-dark-suspense-and-intense-fight-choreography
- Production notes: https://about.netflix.com/en/news/the-shadow-strays-becomes-a-global-hit-trending-in-85-countries-on-netflix
- Action design: silat, kickboxing, katana work and other weapons are assigned as character-specific movement vocabularies.
- Camera / editing: large action is broken into clear local exchanges and environment-zone changes.
- AI-video takeaway: lock each fighter to one style, weapon and tactical role; never let identities or movement grammar drift randomly.

## Games

### Stellar Blade — Read → Parry/Dodge → Counter Combat Flow
- Source: https://blog.playstation.com/2024/04/08/stellar-blade-interview-creating-stylish-sci-fi-action-in-a-post-apocalyptic-world/
- Boss Challenge: https://blog.playstation.com/2024/05/23/stellar-blade-adds-new-boss-challenge-mode-on-may-24/
- Action design: enemy telegraph → player read → timed parry/dodge → counterattack → reset.
- Camera / editing: readable gameplay presentation preserves anticipation and defensive timing.
- AI-video takeaway: include visible anticipation and recovery; do not make both fighters attack continuously.

### Nioh 3 — Samurai / Ninja Style-Shift Combat
- Source: https://blog.playstation.com/2025/11/21/nioh-3-hands-on-report/
- Additional hands-on: https://blog.playstation.com/2025/09/25/nioh-3-tokyo-game-show-hands-on-report/
- Action design: defensive Samurai deflect → instant style switch → agile Ninja flank/burst → disengage.
- Camera / editing: one stable combat camera makes the tactical state change legible through stance and speed.
- AI-video takeaway: a fight can feel structured by giving the hero one explicit mid-sequence mode change.

## Anime

### Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle — Tanjiro & Giyu vs. Akaza Battle Trailer
- Official trailer hub: https://demonslayer-anime.com/infinitycastle/trailer/
- Source: https://www.crunchyroll.com/news/latest/2025/8/9/demon-slayer-kimetsu-no-yaiba-infinity-castle-battle-trailer-tanjiro-akaza
- Action design: strong approach silhouettes, explosive clashes, short recovery poses and supernatural acceleration.
- Camera / editing: VFX and directional cuts bridge a small number of anchor poses.
- AI-video takeaway: use 3–4 anchors—approach → clash → displaced recovery → finishing attempt—and reserve effects for transitions.

## Martial Arts

### Takeru vs. Rodtang — ONE 172 Full Fight
- Watch: https://www.onefc.com/videos/takeru-vs-rodtang-one-172-full-fight/
- Action design: feints, probing kicks, punching bursts, checks, blocks, retreats and guard resets.
- Camera / editing: sports coverage keeps both fighters, distance and contact points readable.
- AI-video takeaway: include missed/checked attacks and immediate stance recovery; realism comes from failed attempts as much as clean hits.

## Stunt / Choreography

### Wick Is Pain — John Wick Stunt Documentary Trailer
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LaEjB78QLSE
- Official franchise page: https://www.lionsgate.com/franchises/john-wick
- Study: behind-the-scenes visibility into the physical rehearsal and stunt culture behind the John Wick series.
- Camera / editing: useful process reference for designing performer movement and camera route as one coordinated system.
- AI-video takeaway: previs 3–4 repeatable physical beats and one camera path before adding speed, muzzle flash, debris or stylization.

## AI Action

### ReMoGen — Real-Time Human Interaction-to-Reaction Generation (CVPR 2026)
- Project / videos: https://4dvlab.github.io/project_page/remogen/
- Paper / code: https://github.com/4DVLab/ReMoGen
- Action design insight: the responder's motion is conditioned on another person's observed motion rather than generated as an unrelated track.
- AI-video takeaway: author a fight in reactive micro-segments—A commits → B reacts → contact/spacing updates → next segment follows the new state.
