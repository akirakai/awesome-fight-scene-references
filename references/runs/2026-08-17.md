# Curated Fight References — 2026-08-17

Eight non-duplicate references selected for choreography clarity, camera language, physical readability, and usefulness as AI-video reference material.

## Film / TV

### Daredevil: Born Again Season 2 — Northern Star Ship Sequence
- Source: https://www.marvel.com/articles/tv-shows/marvel-television-daredevil-born-again-season-2-premiere-breakdown
- Why study it: a recent large-scale action opener that combines infiltration, close-quarters fighting, a sinking environment and water stunts without losing the hero objective.
- Action design: short tactical encounters are chained to environmental state changes; the fight is never just a flat exchange because the ship itself becomes the escalating threat.
- Camera / editing: spectacle expands from tight interior combat to wider disaster imagery while preserving forward mission direction.
- AI-video takeaway: for a 15-second set piece, structure the environment as an escalation engine: enter → fight → environment destabilizes → forced escape. Give the camera one clear subject objective throughout.
- Tags: ship-fight, environmental-escalation, infiltration, close-quarters, 2026

### The Man from Toronto — Barbell Brawl
- Source: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/inside-the-epic-barbell-brawl-of-the-man-from-toronto
- Why study it: a useful example of building a climactic fight around one location-specific prop system instead of generic punching.
- Action design: barbells, gym equipment and changing opponents create successive micro-problems, so the choreography keeps changing vocabulary as the fighters move through the set.
- Camera / editing: the filmmakers vary scale and angle by prop interaction while keeping the protagonist easy to track through the chaos.
- AI-video takeaway: choose one environment theme and assign one action idea to each prop station: rack → bench → barbell → machine → exit. This is more stable than asking an AI model for an undifferentiated “epic gym fight.”
- Tags: gym-fight, improvised-weapons, environmental-action, prop-choreography

## Games

### NINJA GAIDEN 4 — High-Speed Sword Combat Flow
- Source: https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/08/07/ninja-gaiden-4-deep-immersive-allure/
- Why study it: Team NINJA and PlatinumGames design the encounter around sustained pressure, instant weapon switching and a constant feeling of adversity rather than isolated flashy finishers.
- Action design: enemy pressure, evasive movement, weapon swaps and Bloodraven transitions create a clear loop of defend → reposition → transform → reverse momentum.
- Camera / editing: the gameplay camera remains readable even at very high speed by maintaining threat direction and a consistent player anchor.
- AI-video takeaway: for fast sword action, do not prompt nonstop maximum-speed movement. Use one readable base stance, one burst of acceleration, one weapon/state change, then one decisive reversal.
- Tags: ninja, sword-combat, high-speed, weapon-switch, momentum-reversal

### Where Winds Meet — Wuxia Umbrella Combat & Motion Capture
- Source: https://blog.playstation.com/2025/09/25/how-where-winds-meets-wuxia-inspired-combat-turns-the-umbrella-into-a-weapon/
- Video gallery / BTS: https://www.playstation.com/en-in/games/where-winds-meet/
- Why study it: the team worked with veteran Hong Kong action director Stephen Tung Wai and captured moves with interacting stunt performers rather than isolated solo animation.
- Action design: the umbrella changes role—shield, closed striking weapon, ranged tool, mobility device—so one prop generates multiple distinct combat states.
- Camera / editing: full-body paired mocap preserves reaction timing and spatial awareness before fantasy amplification is added.
- AI-video takeaway: a strong wuxia prompt pattern is physical base action first, signature prop transformation second, supernatural amplification last. Preserve the opponent reaction and contact timing through all three phases.
- Tags: wuxia, umbrella, hong-kong-action, paired-mocap, fantasy-combat

## Anime

### LAZARUS — Axel Action Trailer
- Source / watch: https://lazarus.aniplex.co.jp/
- Why study it: the official site includes an Axel Action Trailer, with action supervision by Chad Stahelski and 87Eleven Action Design, making it a rare direct bridge between live-action stunt language and anime movement.
- Action design: parkour-like locomotion, close fighting and evasive movement are staged as character behavior rather than disconnected spectacle.
- Camera / editing: strong directional movement and clean pose silhouettes let the anime exaggerate distance and speed without losing the action path.
- AI-video takeaway: borrow the live-action logic first—approach, contact, recovery, escape line—then exaggerate only distance, hang time or speed. Keep every frame tied to the character’s objective.
- Tags: anime-action, 87eleven, chad-stahelski, parkour, character-driven-action

## Stunt / Choreography

### Avatar: The Last Airbender — Aang vs. King Bumi Fight BTS
- Source / watch: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/videos/avatar-the-last-airbender-season-1-aang-king-bumi-fight-behind-the-scenes
- Why study it: one of the clearest official demonstrations of stunt choreography, VFX and camera planning being developed together months before the final sequence.
- Action design: performers execute understandable martial-arts beats while VFX is attached to specific strikes, light cues and body motions; Aang’s movement foundation includes bagua and wushu.
- Camera / editing: stunt and VFX versions are combined into a hybrid previs/edit before final production, creating a concrete blueprint for every effect-driven beat.
- AI-video takeaway: ideal workflow reference for fantasy combat: choreograph a physically valid move → assign the exact effect trigger → lock camera timing → only then amplify the effect. Do not generate body action and magic as unrelated layers.
- Tags: fantasy-fight, wushu, bagua, vfx-previs, effect-trigger, live-action

## Martial Arts / Performance

### Superlek vs. Rui Botelho — Muay Thai Masterclass
- Short study cut: https://www.onefc.com/videos/class-is-in-session-relive-the-muay-thai-masterclass-between-superlek-and-rui-botelho-in-2019/
- Full fight: https://www.onefc.com/videos/superlek-vs-rui-botelho-one-championship-full-fight/
- Why study it: excellent real-world reference for pressure fighting, kick range, guard recovery, feints and repeated body-position resets.
- Action design: Superlek advances behind compact pressure and varied kicks while Botelho must repeatedly regain stance and range; not every attack lands, which makes the rhythm believable.
- Camera / editing: neutral ring coverage keeps weight transfer, distance and defensive reactions fully visible.
- AI-video takeaway: for photorealistic striking, include missed or checked attacks and visible resets. A reliable 5-second module is probe → low/body kick → guard recovery → opponent retreats → re-entry.
- Tags: muay-thai, pressure-fighting, kicking, stance-recovery, realistic-combat

## AI Action

### CoShMDM — Contact- and Shape-Aware Two-Person Motion Generation
- Project / videos: https://alimanjotho.github.io/coshmdm/
- Why study it: the public project includes generated boxing, pushing and knockdown interactions while explicitly modeling mesh-level contact and penetration avoidance.
- Action design: examples expose whether the attacker’s motion actually produces a corresponding defensive or displaced reaction in the second body.
- Camera / editing: clean renderings make contact errors visible instead of hiding them behind cinematic cuts.
- AI-video takeaway: useful for diagnosing two-character fight failures. Treat contact as a constraint: define which body parts touch, when contact happens, which direction force travels, and what recovery/displacement follows immediately afterward.
- Tags: ai-motion, two-person, boxing, contact-modeling, penetration-avoidance, interaction
