## Martial Arts

### Zahra Kiani — 17th World Wushu Championships Women’s Qiangshu Gold Medal Performance

- Category: Martial Arts / Spear / Taolu
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aT9AuVXdzqc
- Source: WUSHU TV (IWUF WUSHU OFFICIAL)
- Why it is worth studying: A clean full-body spear-form reference with very little cinematic interference, making the body-to-weapon mechanics easy to read.
- Action design: low stance and weight transfer → hip/shoulder rotation → rear-hand drive and front-hand guidance → long spear-tip arc → step/turn → recovery. The weapon trajectory clearly follows the body rather than floating independently.
- Camera / editing: stable competition framing preserves feet, torso, hands, shaft and spear tip at the same time; there is almost no edit-driven acceleration.
- AI-video takeaways: excellent motion reference for a female spear fighter. Prompt the kinetic chain from feet and hips outward to the spear, then add costume, hair, particles and cinematic camera motion only after the base body mechanics are locked.
- Tags: wushu, qiangshu, spear, female-fighter, full-body, weapon-trajectory, motion-reference

## Film / TV

### The Man from Nowhere — Karambit Knife Duel / Scenic Fights Breakdown

- Category: Film / Knife Fight / Close Quarters
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nKP_iySGGuI
- Companion melee breakdown: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ioUBE7P7TNQ
- Why it is worth studying: One of the clearest references for short-range blade logic: hand fighting, angle changes, checking the weapon arm and immediate counterattacks all happen inside a very small radius.
- Action design: threat hand enters → defender redirects/checks → body angle changes → short countercut/thrust → opponent recoils or reorients → immediate re-engagement. The duel avoids wide ornamental swings and keeps intent readable.
- Camera / editing: medium-close coverage stays close enough to feel dangerous while still showing shoulders, elbows and weapon hands; cuts tend to follow tactical changes rather than hiding every contact.
- AI-video takeaways: useful for 6–12 second knife exchanges. Explicitly assign attacking hand, defending hand, blade side and contact order. Keep both weapons continuously visible and avoid asking the model for long spinning combos.
- Tags: knife-fight, karambit, fma, close-quarters, hand-control, contact-order, tactical-duel

- Category: Games / Party Combat / Boss Staging

- Why it is worth studying: The ten-minute deep dive shows large-boss staging, seamless character switching and role-specific combat through the new FITS system, including Knight and Summoner-style behaviors.

## Anime

### One Punch Man — Saitama vs. Boros

- Category: Anime / Extreme-Speed Combat / Power Escalation
- Watch: https://www.bilibili.tv/en/video/2042947980
- Why it is worth studying: A classic example of escalating from readable melee exchanges to absurd speed and scale without losing the core attacker–defender relationship.
- Action design: clear setup pose → explosive straight-line entry → impact/displacement → brief orientation reset → larger-scale retaliation → decisive final action. The sequence repeatedly returns to readable poses between extreme bursts.
- Camera / editing: very wide scale-establishing shots alternate with tight impact shots; extreme acceleration is often bridged by smear/energy frames rather than by making every pose equally detailed.
- AI-video takeaways: for anime-style speed, lock 4–6 anchor poses and let motion blur, debris, energy trails and camera travel connect them. Do not describe twenty attacks individually; preserve one tactical idea per burst.
- Tags: anime, super-speed, power-escalation, anchor-poses, impact, scale-change, motion-blur

`attacker commits one weapon hand → defender redirects/checks → angle change → short counter → separation of half a step → re-entry`

`physical starter → magic extension → companion/beast assist → visible enemy displacement → short ranged/area finish → recovery pose`

### Multi-character boss fight
`boss telegraphs → active attacker enters → support character adds one synergy beat → boss reacts/repositions → camera re-establishes geography → next active character`

### Extreme-speed anime fight
`anchor pose → explosive travel → impact pose → displacement → orientation reset → stronger counter / finish`

`body trajectory → contact timing → target order → screen direction → camera side → recovery state → transition into the next clip`
