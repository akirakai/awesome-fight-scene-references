## Review checklist / sampling protocol

1. Choose a **single source clip**, and crop a continuous 2–5 s action phrase where possible (not a trailer montage or a stitched model comparison).
2. Annotate fighter A/B's on-screen side, active limb/weapon ownership, contact moment, weight transfer, and final recovery pose.
3. Generate a **fixed-wide baseline** first, then separately rerun the same choreography with a limited moving camera. Do not change action and camera simultaneously during fidelity evaluation.
4. For AI comparison videos, preserve the original creator link and model/version labels; never attribute the example English prompt above to the creator.
5. Score contact, body topology, grounding, spatial axis, prop/weapon scale, and identity continuity independently.
