## Recommended 15-second breakdown

- **0–3s:** Establish screen direction, the goal and two combatant positions (the official *The Gray Man* example is especially useful for object-driven motivation).
- **3–8s:** One physically continuous exchange from Olympics / Muay Thai / *REWIND*; hold body framing through recovery.
- **8–12s:** Add one distinctive motion rule (sword clash, boss telegraph or qinggong leap) but keep movement and prop ownership anchored.
- **12–15s:** Motivate one cut or camera reposition, then end on a readable recovery / new state. For the *Predator* reel, plan the fighter blocking before the camera path.

**Quality-control checklist:** fighter identity, left-right axis, hand-to-prop ownership, contact points, center of mass, start/end trajectory, editorial continuity and rights/provenance.
