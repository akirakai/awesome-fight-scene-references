## martial arts

### Nicholas Maskhulia vs Ramini Feradze — Eurasian Professional Sanda League

- **Watch / 原片:** https://euwushu.tv/videos/nicholas-maskhulia-vs-ramini-feradze
- **Source / 日期:** WUSHU.TV direct match video, 9:48; match 2026-02-14 in Tbilisi, published 2026-02-16.
- **Choreography / 动作:** 真实散打双方在有效距离外试探，突然进身后以格挡、退出攻击线、复位组成短攻防；挑选能看清支撑脚的完整回合。
- **Camera & edit / 运镜:** 赛事转播机位；不把多回合的剪辑误当连续动作。适合分析距离和重心，而非追求复杂绕拍。
- **AI takeaway / 可复用元素:** 两名真人角色不要同时无休止出拳：给每一次攻击明确的防守结果和支撑脚回收。
- **Suggested motion crop / 建议裁取:** 3–5s, one exchange
- **Prompt pattern (original; not creator-supplied):** Stable full-body ringside shot. Fighter A steps into range with one kick; fighter B checks it, circles half a step, and both reset their guards. Keep feet visible and contact readable.
- **Tags:** sanda,grounded-footwork,defense-reset,full-body

### Turkish Jiti Team Performance — traditional group wushu

- **Watch / 原片:** https://euwushu.tv/public/videos/turkish-jiti-team-performance
- **Source / 日期:** WUSHU.TV 5:09 direct video, published 2026-02-09; group taolu performance, **not** unscripted sparring.
- **Choreography / 动作:** 多人集体武术的同时起势、同步方向变化与收势；研究多人编舞的时序而非真实攻击命中。
- **Camera & edit / 运镜:** 以全景为主观察整体队形是否整齐，换位时保持观众对群体朝向的认知。
- **AI takeaway / 可复用元素:** 适合 AI 多角色群像中的同步动作/错峰运动：先固定每人起始位置，再加入一次队形调整。
- **Suggested motion crop / 建议裁取:** 3–6s, synchronized beat
- **Prompt pattern (original; not creator-supplied):** Wide static shot of a five-person original wushu troupe. All performers synchronize one step-and-strike form, stagger their turn by a beat, then settle into a clean tableau; no overlapping limbs.
- **Tags:** jiti,group-choreography,synchronization,formation

## Suggested 15-second assembly

0–3s: stable establishment of characters, props and orientation. 3–7s: one complete attack→defense. 7–11s: reposition with a clear camera trajectory. 11–15s: counter/finisher and visible recovery. Keep camera motion, performer movement and style independent until continuity is verified.
