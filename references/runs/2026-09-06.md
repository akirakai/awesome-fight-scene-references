## Film / TV

### Upgrade (2018) — The Kitchen Fight

- Category: Film / AI-Assisted Close Quarters
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AZxn9md_aZI
- Why it is worth studying: The sequence has a deliberately unusual body-control grammar: Grey’s conscious facial reaction and STEM-driven physical response feel slightly disconnected, yet the actual strikes, pivots and balance changes remain easy to read.
- Action design: sudden mechanical pivots, efficient counters, low-waste body turns and abrupt changes in attack direction create a distinctive “machine controls a human body” style without abandoning physical contact logic.
- Camera / editing: the camera follows the torso and orientation changes aggressively while still holding long enough to register the start, contact and recovery of each major beat.
- AI-video takeaways: useful for possessed, augmented or remotely controlled characters. Separate body state from expression: threat appears → body snaps into precise counter → contact → mechanically clean recovery → face reacts a beat later.
- Tags: upgrade, kitchen-fight, augmented-human, mechanical-motion, close-quarters, body-control

### Re:Born — Zero Range Combat

- Category: Film / Zero-Range Close Quarters
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uxM6vsMNwXs
- Production / action context: https://www.udenflameworks.com/reborn/interview.html
- Why it is worth studying: Re:Born builds its action around keeping distance extremely short, which naturally reduces the sprawling footwork and random attack choices that often destabilize AI-generated fights.
- Action design: Toshiro repeatedly closes inside an opponent’s usable weapon range, redirects limbs, uses compact knife/hand movements and sometimes turns one opponent into a shield or obstacle against another.
- Camera / editing: close-to-medium framing stays near the protagonist while the choreography itself creates target handoffs; the body remains the main source of speed instead of relying only on cuts.
- AI-video takeaways: strong template for a 10–15 second “one fighter vs several” sequence: close gap → trap/redirect current threat → use that body as spatial cover → pivot to next threat → reset at zero range.
- Tags: re-born, tak-sakaguchi, zero-range-combat, close-quarters, target-handoff, compact-choreography

## Prompt patterns distilled from this batch

1. **Three-person action:** one active pair + one timed interrupter; never let all three improvise simultaneously.
2. **Machine-controlled body:** expression and body state can diverge, but contact physics and recovery must stay causal.
3. **Zero-range crowd fight:** close gap → control current threat → use that body/environment as cover → hand off to next threat.
4. **High-mobility mecha:** explicit locomotion state → committed attack → recoil/reposition → explicit weapon-state change.
5. **Anime extreme speed:** readable anchor poses first; smears, speed ramps and elemental VFX only bridge the anchors.
6. **Reference-video pipeline:** choreography first, camera second, appearance/style last; do not make the text prompt compete with motion reference.
7. **AI action evaluation:** body trajectory, interaction causality, identity/weapon consistency and camera adherence should be scored separately.
