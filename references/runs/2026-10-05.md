# 2026-10-05 Fight Scene References

Curated references for AI video generation. Candidates were checked against this repository before adding. Priority: readable movement, useful camera language, clear rhythm, and prompt-friendly structure.

## martial arts

### Asian Games 2026 — Lin Yu-ting vs. Yang Chengyu, Women's 60kg Boxing Final
- Source: https://focustaiwan.tw/video/004356744
- Date: 2026-10-02
- Type: Boxing / real competition
- Why study it: Excellent reach management, patient distance control, and clear tempo changes.
- Action design: distance read → lead-hand probe → short exchange → angle exit → guard reset.
- Camera / editing: Stable broadcast coverage preserves both fighters' feet, stance width, and ring position.
- AI-video takeaway: Include pauses and resets; one short committed exchange per beat is more believable than nonstop motion.
- Tags: boxing, range-control, footwork, real-competition

## film-TV

### Police Story (1985) — Mall Fight
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DvWNIIEovvU
- Type: Film / environmental action
- Why study it: The location is part of the choreography; railings, stairs, display areas, and glass structures become spatial anchors.
- Action design: opponent enters → evade / redirect → environment interaction → clear displacement → move to the next spatial node.
- Camera / editing: Wide and medium-wide framing shows full-body movement and object interaction before cutting.
- AI-video takeaway: Define 3–5 environmental anchors and assign one interaction to each beat.
- Tags: Jackie-Chan, environment-choreography, group-action, full-body

### The Raid: Redemption — Second Hallway Fight
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2x0jCO9RZDs
- Type: Film / corridor group action
- Why study it: Strong reference for threat sequencing and readable depth in a narrow space.
- Action design: corridor advance → front opponent commits → redirect / counter → clear position change → next opponent replaces the threat from depth.
- Camera / editing: A strong single depth axis keeps geography readable.
- AI-video takeaway: Lock the corridor axis and use one active opponent at a time, with the next threat visibly queued deeper in frame.
- Tags: hallway, depth-axis, close-quarters, one-active-opponent

## anime

### Samurai Jack — Season 1 Fight Compilation
- Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wO-davXntX4
- Type: Animation / stylized sword and group action
- Why study it: Excellent silhouette-first staging. Fast movement remains readable because the key poses are simple and unmistakable.
- Action design: ready pose → directional attack → evasive shape → short pause → counter / displacement → new silhouette.
- Camera / editing: Bold wides, graphic compositions, directional cuts, and short holds on key poses.
- AI-video takeaway: Lock 3–5 anchor poses and let the speed happen between them.
- Tags: Samurai-Jack, silhouette, anchor-pose, readable-speed

## stunt-choreography

### ALL-IN — Action Short Film
- Source: https://vimeo.com/54123434
- Type: Stunt / action short
- Why study it: Winner of Best Fight Choreography (Short) at the 2012 Action On Film Festival; movement coverage is performance-led rather than effect-led.
- Action design: grounded exchange → close pressure → positional reversal → brief recovery → renewed exchange.
- Camera / editing: Coverage stays close enough for impact while preserving full-body mechanics.
- AI-video takeaway: Useful 2–5 second V2V reference. Let the source clip control body trajectory, facing, and timing while prompt / character references control identity and setting.
- Tags: stunt, choreography, V2V, grounded-action

## AI-action

- Source: https://www.bilibili.tv/video/4800757294045696
- Date: 2026-08-29
- Type: AI Action / Seedance 2.5
- Why study it: A compact 30-second sequence that treats timing, speed ramps, slow motion, dynamic camera movement, effects, and impact as separate directing controls.
