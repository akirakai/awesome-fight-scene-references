Seven newly screened references for AI-video action design. This batch was checked against the repository for title-level duplication before writing. It mixes fresh 2026 competition/game/AI material with classic sword choreography, an award-winning action short, a readable ensemble anime fight, and a compact stunt short.

## Martial Arts

### Muju Taekwondowon 2026 World Taekwondo Grand Prix — M-58kg Golden Moments

- Category: Martial Arts / Taekwondo / Live Combat
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CmxWwS1k1hk
- Source: World Taekwondo official
- Published: 2026-09-06
- Why it is worth studying: elite taekwondo gives clean real-world examples of distance management, sudden linear entries, chambered kicks, spinning attacks, evasive exits and immediate stance recovery. The highlight format is dense enough to mine short motion references without losing the authentic attack-response rhythm.
- Action design: probe / bounce → range close → single committed kick or short kick sequence → defender check, lean-back or retreat → occasional spin / counter → separation → stance reset.
- Camera / editing: broadcast-wide framing usually keeps both athletes, their feet and the scoring line visible, which makes trajectory and contact timing easy to inspect.
- AI-video takeaways: for believable kicking combat, preserve the preparation and landing. Prompt the chamber, hip turn, extension, contact or miss, retraction and foot recovery instead of only naming the kick.
- Tags: taekwondo, world-taekwondo, grand-prix, 2026, kicking, distance-management, spinning-kick, realistic-combat

## Film / TV

### Zatoichi Challenged — Zatoichi Sword Duel

- Category: Film / TV / Classic Samurai Duel
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Hvc1Ae-YCU
- Reference page: https://apexkrav.com/fight-scene-of-the-week-zatoichi-challenged/
- Film: 1967
- Why it is worth studying: the scene uses long buildup and restraint before an extremely short decisive burst. It is almost the opposite of modern nonstop sword choreography, which makes the acceleration feel much more dangerous.
- Action design: measured approach / stillness → tiny posture read → explosive committed draw / cut → immediate consequence → stillness again. The tension comes from delayed commitment, not move count.
- Camera / editing: wide, relatively static framing avoids rapid cutting and keeps both fighters' spacing and blade lines readable.
- AI-video takeaways: excellent template for a 6–10 second samurai duel. Spend most of the shot on controlled anticipation, then use one fast attack-response exchange and a clear final silhouette instead of stacking many sword moves.
- Tags: zatoichi, samurai, sword-duel, iaijutsu, anticipation, burst-action, classic-cinema

### BRAWLER — Martial Arts Action Short Film (4K)

- Category: Film / TV / Martial Arts Short Film
- Watch: https://vimeo.com/949671007
- Credits: directed by Bulent Ozdemir-Larusso; stunt coordinator/action director Lukaz Leong; fight choreography by Lukaz Leong and Isky Fay.
- Recognition: winner of Best British Short Film and Best Action Choreography at Fighting Spirit Film Festival 2024.
- Why it is worth studying: a modern action short built around choreography as the main attraction, with enough production polish to study both body mechanics and cinematic presentation. It is especially useful as a bridge between raw stunt-vis and finished film language.
- Action design: compact close-range exchanges → opponent displacement → short reposition → renewed attack → reversal / finish. The action is organized in readable phrases rather than one uninterrupted blur.
- Camera / editing: camera placement stays close enough for impact but regularly returns to wider body-readable angles; cuts are motivated by a new tactical beat or direction change.
- AI-video takeaways: good reference for turning a 2–5 second stunt phrase into a finished shot. Lock body trajectory and contact first, then add one camera move, impact shake and environmental reaction without changing the choreography.
- Tags: brawler, action-short, martial-arts, fight-choreography, 4k, stunt-vis-to-film, close-quarters

## Games

### Onimusha: Way of the Sword — Swordplay Action Trailer

- Category: Games / Samurai Sword Combat / Reactive Duel
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B1GSlvemBxM
- Launch trailer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cm4slr3UKOQ
- Official combat reference: https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/09/03/onimusha-way-of-the-sword-tips-guide-combat/
- Published: swordplay trailer 2026-08-31; game released 2026-09-04.
- Why it is worth studying: the combat is built around answering an incoming attack with a specific defensive/offensive response rather than simply maintaining a combo. Parry, Deflect and the high-risk Issen counter create visually clear cause-and-effect sword exchanges.
- Action design: enemy telegraph → guard / parry / deflect decision → steel-on-steel contact → enemy stamina / posture break → immediate counter or Issen → short reset. Projectile deflection adds a second readable response grammar.
- Camera / editing: third-person framing keeps Musashi and the current threat locked in a clear axis; impact sparks and hit reactions emphasize the exact contact frame instead of hiding it.
- AI-video takeaways: strong template for AI sword fights: write each exchange as attacker intent → defender response → blade contact → recoil / stagger → counter. Avoid both fighters independently swinging at the same time.
- Tags: onimusha, 2026, samurai, parry, deflect, issen, swordplay, attack-response

## Anime

### Mob Psycho 100 II — Shimazaki vs. Everyone

- Category: Anime / Ensemble Fight / Teleportation Combat
- Watch episode: https://www.crunchyroll.com/watch/GYMEWV5G6/guidance-psychic-sensor
- Crunchyroll animation feature: https://www.crunchyroll.com/th/news/features/2022/12/29/feature-the-10-best-animation-moments-of-mob-psycho-100
- Why it is worth studying: the fight solves a hard staging problem: one teleporting fighter versus several opponents with different powers. Even at very high speed, the sequence repeatedly re-establishes who is attacking, where Shimazaki reappears and which defender responds.
- Action design: target appears → Shimazaki instant reposition → single decisive strike / evade → another esper intercepts → brief team counterattack → teleport reset → escalation to Mind's Eye. Each participant retains a distinct technique.
- Camera / editing: fast camera movement follows the teleport vectors, but the animation repeatedly lands on readable silhouettes before the next burst. Crunchyroll specifically highlights the fast-paced camera work and the way each esper's individual technique remains visible.
- AI-video takeaways: for teleportation or super-speed fights, do not ask for continuous random warping. Define explicit anchor positions and target handoffs: position A attack → vanish → position B impact → team response → position C reset.
- Tags: mob-psycho-100, shimazaki, teleportation, ensemble-fight, super-speed, target-handoff, sakuga

## Stunt / Choreography

### HALL FIGHT — A One Minute Action Film

- Category: Stunt / Choreography / Short Fight Film
- Watch: https://vimeo.com/368733368
- Credits: fight choreographer Bronwin October; stunts and safety Matt Esof; written/directed/edited by David Wayne Smith.
- Recognition: winner of Best Film (Action), One Minute Film Festival South Africa.
- Why it is worth studying: one minute forces the action to be compressed into clear, complete phrases. The sequence is especially useful for studying how a female lead can handle multiple attackers without turning the shot into an unreadable crowd brawl.
- Action design: threat enters → short exchange → decisive displacement → target switch → reversal → new threat. The choreography prioritizes complete actions and clean handoffs over excessive move density.
- Camera / editing: concise coverage and motivated reframing keep the protagonist as the anchor while different attackers enter and leave the active pair.
- AI-video takeaways: very suitable for 10–15 second generation. Use one active attacker at a time and give each exchange a beginning, contact beat and recovery before handing the scene to the next opponent.
- Tags: hall-fight, stunt-choreography, one-minute-film, female-action, multi-attacker, target-handoff

## AI Action

### MiniMax H3 — “超然打斗” Skill / High-Dynamic Fight Demo

- Category: AI Action / MiniMax H3 / Prompted Combat
- Watch: https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1sotB6dEz3/
- Published: 2026-09-04
- Why it is worth studying: the video is specifically focused on high-dynamic H3 fight generation and presents both generated fight examples and a reusable action-prompting skill/workflow. It is more useful as a diagnostic reference than a pure beauty reel because the emphasis is on impact and action wording.
- Action design / evaluation focus: attack initiation, opponent response, visible contact, recoil, follow-through, reposition and second attack. The useful question is whether each action phrase produces a causal reaction rather than two independent animations.
- Camera / editing: the demo leans into aggressive fight-game / cinematic motion, so it is useful for separating camera energy from choreography quality. A strong result should remain readable even with the camera movement mentally removed.
- AI-video takeaways: score the generation in layers: 1) body trajectory, 2) contact timing and recoil, 3) opponent relationship / screen direction, 4) camera energy, 5) image polish. Do not use whip-pan or shake to disguise broken contact.
- Tags: minimax-h3, ai-action, fight-prompting, high-dynamic-action, impact, contact-timing, workflow, 2026
