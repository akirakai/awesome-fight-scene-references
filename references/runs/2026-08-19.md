# Curated Fight References — 2026-08-19

Seven new, non-duplicate references selected for practical AI action-video study.

## Film / TV

### The Gray Man — Dhanush vs. Sierra Six & Dani
- Watch: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/videos/dhanush-fights-ryan-gosling-and-ana-de-armas-the-gray-man
- Action breakdown: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/videos/deep-dive-into-the-action-scenes-in-the-gray-man
- Why study it: a very readable two-versus-one fight where the objective and power balance remain clear.
- Action design: the stronger solo fighter repeatedly breaks the pair apart, forcing them to re-enter instead of letting all three attack simultaneously.
- Camera / editing: medium and medium-wide coverage keeps the active attacker and defender legible; cuts mostly follow changes in tactical control.
- AI-video takeaway: structure 2v1 prompts as alternating pressure windows: A attacks C → C disrupts A → B enters → C redirects → A returns. Keep only one dominant contact pair at a time.
- Tags: 2v1, tactical-fight, readable-action, role-switching, netflix

## Stunt / Choreography

### Stranger Things 5 — Karen vs. Demogorgon Behind the Scenes
- Watch: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/videos/stranger-scenes-chapter-two
- Why study it: excellent reference for a human fighting a CG creature in a familiar domestic space, including kitchen interaction, eyelines and practical contact proxies.
- Action design: the human performer reacts to a fixed creature position, uses a household object as a weapon, and plays every contact against practical marks or stand-ins.
- Camera / editing: the sequence was storyboarded and previsualized; creature height, eyelines and Steadicam height were solved before VFX replacement.
- AI-video takeaway: for human-vs-creature generation, lock creature scale and position first, then define contact points and performer eyelines. Use one practical environment object per action beat.
- Tags: creature-fight, kitchen, previs, eyeline, practical-vfx, human-vs-monster

### The Recruit Season 2 — Behind the Action
- Watch: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/videos/go-behind-the-scenes-of-the-recruit-season-2
- Why study it: useful actor-performance reference for grounded spy fights where choreography is learned as short countable phrases before emotion and urgency are layered in.
- Action design: compact hand-to-hand beats, improvised-threat moments and physical reactions are rehearsed like short dance phrases.
- Camera / editing: coverage favors clear body mechanics and lets the performer carry continuity across short stunt modules.
- AI-video takeaway: write a 15-second sequence as 3–4 countable movement phrases, then add urgency, facial performance and camera travel after the physical order is fixed.
- Tags: spy-action, stunt-training, counted-choreography, grounded-fight, actor-performance

## Martial Arts

### Rodtang vs. Jiduo Yibu — Kickboxing Full Fight
- Watch: https://www.onefc.com/videos/supreme-power-rodtang-vs-jiduo-yibu-kickboxing-full-fight/
- Why study it: clean full-body competition footage with real probing, guard changes, pressure, checks, misses and stance recovery.
- Action design: feint/probe → short combination → block or partial miss → angle change → reset.
- Camera / editing: fixed sports coverage keeps both fighters, distance and contact points visible, making it excellent pose/reference material.

- Project / demos: https://aigc-explorer.github.io/TIMotion-page/
- Why study it: the public demos include two-person sparring and expose timing/spacing quality without cinematic editing hiding interaction errors.
