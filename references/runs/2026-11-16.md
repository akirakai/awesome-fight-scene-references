## Practical workflow / suggested 15-second experiment

1. **0–3s**: wide geographic establishing shot, label fighter A/B and fixed props.
2. **3–7s**: one attacker initiates; the defender performs a single explicit response in the same 180-degree screen axis.
3. **7–11s**: visible contact or clean miss, followed by a believable displacement and recovery.
4. **11–15s**: reaction and result, with no unexplained character/weapon changes.

Use a static full-body baseline first and only then change the camera; compare pose, weapon ownership, foot placement, contact-to-reaction timing, character identity and geography. For AI shorts, prefer generated original characters and treat real-person likenesses as a separate rights issue.
