## Martial Arts / Performance

### 17th World Wushu Championships — Taolu FOP1 Day 3 Session 6: Daoshu

- Category: Martial Arts / Daoshu / Weapon Form
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Obl3fIRp210
- Why it is worth studying: Official WUSHU TV coverage from the 17th World Wushu Championships, with clean competition framing and elite broadsword body mechanics.
- Action design: Low stance and weight transfer → hip/shoulder rotation → committed dao cut → direction change or leap → controlled landing/recovery. The blade follows the body's center rather than moving independently.
- Camera / editing: Neutral full-body sports coverage keeps feet, torso, weapon arc and landing visible at the same time, making it unusually useful as pure motion reference.
- AI-video takeaways: Build sword prompts from the body outward: stance/weight shift → torso rotation → arm/blade arc → landing/reset. Add cloth, hair, sparks and cinematic camera movement only after the movement chain is stable.
- Tags: daoshu, wushu, broadsword, weapon-form, full-body-reference, body-mechanics

### Flash Point (2007) — Donnie Yen vs. Collin Chou Final Fight

- Category: Film / TV / MMA-Influenced Close Combat
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C3LQ55qkw-A
- Why it is worth studying: A classic screen fight that mixes striking, takedowns, joint manipulation and hard environmental impacts while keeping the exchange physically readable.
- Action design: Strike entry → clinch/control → throw or takedown → scramble/recovery → renewed striking pressure. The fight repeatedly changes level and range, which prevents it from becoming a flat punch/kick combo reel.
- Camera / editing: Fluid medium and medium-wide coverage favors body readability and sustained exchanges; intensity comes from committed performance and contact rather than shaky camera or hyper-fragmented cutting.
- AI-video takeaways: Excellent grounded duel grammar: 2–3 strike burst → grapple/takedown state change → visible recovery → re-entry. Preserve the reset and body repositioning after throws instead of teleporting directly into the next attack.
- Tags: flash-point, donnie-yen, mma, takedown, grapple, grounded-action, classic-action

## Anime

### Mob Psycho 100 II — Shimazaki vs. Everyone

- Category: Anime / Multi-Fighter Psychic Combat
- Watch: https://www.crunchyroll.com/watch/GYMEWV5G6/guidance-psychic-sensor
- Why it is worth studying: One of the clearest examples of extremely fast multi-character action that remains readable because the tactical relationship keeps changing in explicit steps.
- Action design: Shimazaki initially dominates through teleportation; Teru begins reading the pattern; the other espers then counter with distinct individual techniques. The sequence behaves like active threat → pattern read → coordinated counter → new state rather than everyone attacking at once.
- Camera / editing: Fast-paced camera work and teleport-driven reframing still anchor on attack direction, strong silhouettes and the current active pair.
- AI-video takeaways: For 3+ fighters, keep one active attacker/defender relationship at a time, use one interrupter to trigger the next state, and make teleport/cut transitions land on a readable pose. This is a strong template for supernatural team combat without identity chaos.
- Tags: mob-psycho-100, shimazaki, multi-fighter, teleportation, active-pair, anime-action
