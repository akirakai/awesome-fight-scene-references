## Editing guideline

For a 15s reconstruction: 0–3s establish scene and hand-held props; 3–7s one attack-defence cycle; 7–11s reposition with consistent left/right geography; 11–15s decisive exchange and clear finishing pose. Seek licenses for third-party V2V reuse. 3D previews, stunt reels and AI-assisted films are not equivalent to uncut live action.
