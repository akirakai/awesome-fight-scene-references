### 7. ZOMBIE FIGHT CONCEPTS PREVIS — LBP Stunts Chicago

- **动作设计观察：** 多人/类丧尸威胁下，一次只让一名攻击者进入核心接触区，其余敌人用站位制造压力。
- **运镜 / 剪辑：** 空间明确、偏排练感的预演，可研究镜头是否在换攻击目标时跟随主角，而非依靠随机快速切镜。
- **AI 借鉴：** 一个主角、两名不同方位来敌；在同屏确认 A 退出攻击线后才让 B 接近。
- **建议节选：** 3–6 秒单次多人交互/换位。
- **原创英文 Prompt：** > On an empty rehearsal floor, one adult defender faces two costumed stunt attackers. Attacker A advances first; the defender sidesteps and redirects A past the camera. Only after A clears the lane does attacker B approach from screen-left. Continuous medium-wide camera with visibly separate performers and natural footwork.
- **标签：** `lbp-stunts, action-previs, multi-attacker, rehearsal, threat-handoff, spatial-continuity`
