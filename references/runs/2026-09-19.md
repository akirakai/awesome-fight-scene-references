- Category: Animation / Stop-Motion Samurai / Sword Fight
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DpefYPLH67A
- Official project: https://hidari-movie.com/
- Why it is worth studying: The 3.5-minute wooden-puppet pilot combines anime-like speed with stop-motion pose discipline. Because every pose is physically constructed frame by frame, silhouettes, weapon arcs and impact states remain exceptionally clear even during exaggerated action.
- Action design: guarded pose → committed directional attack → parry / displacement → mechanical-prosthetic weapon state → counterattack → strong held impact silhouette.

## Batch takeaways

- **Acrobatic movement:** specify takeoff, airborne trajectory, landing and balance recovery as one chain.
- **Character state changes:** make takeover / transformation visible, then change movement grammar after the transition.
- **Sword fights:** preserve attack line, parry side, blade-hand identity and recovery before adding speed or VFX.
- **Stylized action:** use a small number of strong anchor poses and let motion effects bridge them.
- **AI multi-shot fights:** carry identity, weapon state, screen direction and exit pose forward explicitly between generation modules.
