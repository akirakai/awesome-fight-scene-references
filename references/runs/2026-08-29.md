# Curated Fight References — 2026-08-29

This batch focuses on clips that are especially useful for AI-video prompt design: constrained-space choreography, readable attack/response timing, camera discipline, realistic combat rhythm, and contact-aware physical interaction.

## Film / TV

### Bullet Train — The Wolf Fight
- Category: Film / TV
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8MgE-zBA_Jc
- Source: Sony Pictures Entertainment official clip.
- Why it works: a compact duel inside a train uses the aisle, seats and luggage as spatial constraints, so the choreography stays readable even at high speed.
- Action design: brief weapon/hand exchanges alternate with evasions and prop interaction instead of becoming one uninterrupted combo.
- Camera / editing: the camera respects the carriage axis and changes framing mainly when the tactical relationship changes.
- AI-video takeaway: excellent 10–15 second template for narrow-space action. Lock one movement axis, use 3–4 explicit attack/response beats, and let one prop or obstacle motivate each transition.
- Tags: bullet-train, duel, narrow-space, train-carriage, environmental-action, readable-choreography

### Captain America: The Winter Soldier — Elevator Fight
- Category: Film / TV
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KYATf2A5GCQ
- Source: Marvel Entertainment official clip.
- Why it works: a one-vs-many fight remains understandable because the room is tiny, the hero stays visually anchored, and opponents engage in staggered waves instead of attacking as a single swarm.
- Action design: restraint attempt → short counter → new attacker enters → body-position reversal → decisive break-out.
- Camera / editing: medium and high-angle coverage preserves the hero's position inside the crowded elevator while cutting only when the attack relationship changes.
- AI-video takeaway: for AI one-vs-many, never make everyone attack at once. Keep one focal attacker plus one interrupter, lock the hero near the center, and make each new opponent enter after the previous contact beat resolves.
- Tags: elevator, one-vs-many, constrained-space, staggered-attackers, crowd-control, marvel

### Reacher — Prison Fight
- Category: Film / TV
- Watch: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FTTs-Uzp048
- Source: Prime Video India official clip.
- Why it works: the scene sells a huge power and size mismatch through economy of motion rather than flashy choreography.
- Action design: the protagonist uses short entries, heavy counters, wall/fixture impacts and quick threat removal; attackers arrive one after another instead of forming an unreadable pile.
- Camera / editing: mostly medium coverage keeps body mass, distance and reaction timing visible, which makes the impacts feel heavier.
- AI-video takeaway: useful for a "powerful but grounded" character. Give the lead one stable movement grammar—minimal footwork, short counters, heavy contact—and let opponents provide the motion around them.
- Tags: reacher, prison-fight, power-imbalance, heavy-contact, grounded-action, one-vs-many

### Obliterated — Finale Kitchen Fight
- Category: Film / TV
- Watch: https://www.netflix.com/title/81413928
- Production notes: https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/obliterated-season-1-ending-explained
- Why it works: the kitchen brawl uses food and kitchen objects as story beats, turning environment interaction into part of the character arc rather than decoration.

## AI Action

### PhyGenHOI — Physically-Aware Human-Object Interaction
- Category: AI Action / Research
- Project / videos: https://omerbenishu.github.io/PhyGenHOI/
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30268
- Why it works: the public demos explicitly model punching, kicking and pushing objects with physically consistent contact and object response rather than treating the target as static scenery.
- Motion design: the system synchronizes the human motion so it actually reaches the object, then triggers contact-driven re-simulation so momentum transfer changes the object's motion.
- Camera / editing: clean research visualizations make wind-up, contact frame, follow-through and object reaction easy to inspect without cinematic cutting hiding errors.
- AI-video takeaway: very useful for environmental fight generation. Prompt contact as a causal chain: body winds up → named joint/body part reaches object → contact frame → object moves/deforms in a specified direction → performer follows through and recovers. Do not prompt "punches table and it flies" as a single undifferentiated event.
- Tags: ai-action, human-object-interaction, contact, momentum-transfer, physics, environmental-action, 2026

## Prompt patterns worth reusing from this batch

1. **Constrained-space duel:** lock one axis → 3–4 attack/response beats → one prop-driven transition → short finish.
2. **One-vs-many:** one active attacker + one interrupter → resolve contact → next attacker enters; never activate the whole crowd simultaneously.
3. **State-changing kitchen fight:** setback → environmental interaction → visible state change → comeback burst → new spatial station.
4. **Grounded duel camera rule:** the more complex the body choreography, the simpler and more stable the camera should become.
5. **Realistic momentum reversal:** Fighter B pressures → Fighter A counters → A takes space → B rallies → A retreats/reset.
6. **Physical environment contact:** wind-up → contact → momentum transfer → object response → follow-through/recovery.
