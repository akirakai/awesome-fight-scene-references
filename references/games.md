## Sifu — Kung Fu & Motion Capture Behind the Scenes
- Source: https://www.sifugame.com/behind-the-scene
- Study: how real Pak-Mei Kung Fu practice, combat-design workshops and motion capture are translated into game action.
- Movement design: compact combinations, position changes, environmental use and clear cause/effect between movements.
- Camera: gameplay-oriented framing keeps body mechanics and spacing readable.
- AI-video takeaway: strong reference for grounded martial-arts generation. Prompt explicit movement beats, stance changes and environmental interaction instead of generic “fast kung fu.”

## Phantom Blade Zero — Donnie Yen / Kenji Tanigaki Kung Fu Combat
- Source: https://www.gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/action-rpg-phantom-blade-zero-tapped-john-wick-and-rogue-one-star-donnie-yen-for-its-kung-fu-combat-his-first-video-game-role-since-the-failed-sleeping-dogs-movie/
- Study: a current example of Hong Kong action-film language being translated directly into game combat; Donnie Yen serves as creative consultant and motion-capture performer, working with action director Kenji Tanigaki.
- Movement design: rapid grounded kung-fu exchanges with strong pose silhouettes, counters, weapon transitions and cinematic impact beats.
- Camera: useful reference for combining readable full-body martial arts with aggressive cinematic reframing rather than hiding choreography in chaotic cuts.
- AI-video takeaway: specify a sequence as discrete attack/counter beats, preserve full-body readability during the exchange, then use one deliberate camera acceleration or impact reframe for the finishing beat.
- Tags: kung-fu, hong-kong-action, mocap, game-combat, donnie-yen, cinematic-action

## EA SPORTS UFC 6 — Markerless Motion Capture
- Source: https://www.ea.com/ea-studios/motive/news/ufc-6-developer-qa
- Study: EA captures real fighters with markerless motion capture so signature combinations and individual movement patterns come directly from the athletes.
- Movement design: excellent reference for realistic weight transfer, stance recovery, defensive reactions and fighter-specific rhythm.
- Camera: neutral sports framing keeps biomechanics readable instead of masking them with cinematic cuts.
- AI-video takeaway: useful whenever realism matters more than spectacle. Prompt stance, weight shift, guard recovery and exact combination rhythm rather than just naming a martial art.
- Tags: mma, markerless-mocap, biomechanics, grounded-action, realistic-combat

## SPINE — Gun Fu Motion Capture & Freeflow Combat
- Source: https://steamcommunity.com/app/1731290/allnews/
- Study: the developers describe full-body Xsens capture and professional stunt performers choreographing complete martial-arts-plus-gunplay sequences.
- Movement design: flowing target transitions, close-range strikes, weapon handling and stunt beats are designed as one continuous physical chain.
- Camera: blockbuster-style framing stays mobile while preserving subject continuity.
- AI-video takeaway: for gun-fu generations, write the action as one connected chain—disarm or parry → body turn → close strike → target switch → weapon presentation—instead of independent flashy moves.
- Tags: gun-fu, mocap, freeflow, stunt-performers, target-switching, cinematic-gameplay
